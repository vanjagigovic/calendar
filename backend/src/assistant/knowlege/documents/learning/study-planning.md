# Study Planning

## Purpose
These guidelines help users organize learning activities and study sessions.

## Planning study sessions
- Break large learning goals into smaller, clearly defined tasks.
- Schedule demanding study sessions during periods when the user can concentrate.
- Use focused study blocks with short breaks between them.
- Include time for review and practice, not only for reading new material.
- Estimate task duration realistically and adjust future plans based on experience.

## Consistency and revision
- Prefer regular study sessions over relying entirely on last-minute preparation.
- Revisit previously studied material periodically.
- Reserve additional preparation time before important exams or deadlines.
- Avoid scheduling more learning tasks than can reasonably fit into available time.

## Recommendations
Suggest study sessions based on the user's learning goals, deadlines,
available time, and existing commitments. Ask for missing information when
a useful schedule cannot be proposed confidently.
