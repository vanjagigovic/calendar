# Meeting Guidelines

## Purpose
These guidelines help users plan productive meetings while protecting time for focused work.

## Scheduling meetings
- Define the purpose and expected outcome before scheduling a meeting.
- Invite only people whose participation is necessary.
- Share an agenda in advance when the meeting requires preparation.
- Prefer shorter meetings when the topic does not require a full hour.
- Leave a short buffer between meetings when possible.

## Calendar planning
- Avoid scheduling meetings over existing calendar events.
- Consider participants' working hours and availability.
- Group related meetings together when practical.
- Reserve preparation and follow-up time for important meetings.
- Do not schedule meetings during explicitly blocked focus time unless necessary.

## Recommendations
When suggesting a meeting time, consider existing commitments,
available time slots, preparation time, and the meeting's priority.
