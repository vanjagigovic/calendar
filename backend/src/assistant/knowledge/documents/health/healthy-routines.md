# Healthy Routines

## Purpose
Healthy routines help users create a balanced daily schedule.

## Daily habits
- Encourage consistent sleep and wake times when compatible with the user's needs.
- Leave time for regular meals and breaks.
- Include short movement breaks during long periods of sedentary work.
- Avoid planning every minute of the day without room for unexpected tasks.
- Consider transitions between activities when building a daily schedule.

## Sustainable planning
- Introduce new habits gradually rather than changing the entire routine at once.
- Use recurring reminders for habits the user explicitly wants to maintain.
- Leave flexible time for personal responsibilities and rest.
- Review routines periodically and adjust them when they become impractical.

## Recommendations
Help users find realistic opportunities for breaks, movement, meals, and rest.
Respect their stated preferences and existing commitments instead of assuming
that one daily routine works for everyone.
